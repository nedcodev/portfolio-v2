GTA 6 Leak Explained: What Cyberleek’s 25-Minute “Extended Leek” Shows (No Spoilers)

By Ned · October 9, 2026 · 5 min read

The short version

  • On October 8, the leaker known as Cyberleek posted about 25 minutes of what looks like GTA 6 gameplay.
  • It’s not verified, Rockstar hasn’t commented, and some of it seems to come from a development build.
  • The headline detail is first-person footage, even though Rockstar has said there’s no full first-person mode at launch.
  • The real game is six weeks away: November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

We’re six weeks from one of the most anticipated game launches ever, and GTA 6 just leaked. Again. If you’ve seen “25 minutes of GTA 6 gameplay” all over your feed and you’re wondering what’s actually going on, this is the spoiler-free breakdown. No leaked clips, no story details. Just what happened, who’s behind it, and what’s actually confirmed.

What happened: the “Extended Leek”

On October 8, 2026, a file titled “GTA 6: An Extended Leek” appeared online, attributed to the leaker Cyberleek. Outlets that looked at it describe roughly 25 minutes of footage in a file of about 370 MB, making it the longest drop from this leaker so far.

According to that coverage, the video shows both protagonists, Jason and Lucia, in gameplay, along with story spoilers and mature content. Some clips appear to show a developer menu, including one where a tank is spawned into the world. That’s a strong hint that this is a work-in-progress build, not the version you’ll be playing in November.

Who is Cyberleek?

Cyberleek is an anonymous leaker who started posting GTA 6 clips on August 18, 2026, beginning with short videos of Jason driving, fighting and even playing basketball. They framed the leaks as a protest against digital-only games, pre-orders and monetization.

There was a twist, though. The leaks were tied to a crypto token, $CYBERLEEK, that fans used to vote on which clip came next. Kotaku and Tom’s Hardware both reported that around $250,000 was cashed out of the coin in late August, right before Rockstar’s own showcase, and the token crashed. After that, Cyberleek went quiet… until this week.

Take-Two isn’t taking it lightly either. It has reportedly gone to court to try to identify whoever runs the accounts.

Does GTA 6 have a first-person mode?

This is the part everyone’s arguing about. The leaked video reportedly shows first-person driving and walking around on foot. Some outlets ran with “first-person mode confirmed.”

Here’s the official word: Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson has said GTA 6 won’t have a full first-person mode at launch, though you can still use a first-person view when aiming some weapons. Footage from a development build doesn’t change that. Features get tested, cut and added later all the time. GTA 5 itself only got first-person on the newer consoles, a year after it first came out.

My take: plan on third-person at launch. If first-person shows up later, that’s a bonus.

Is the leak real?

Honestly, nobody outside Rockstar can say for sure. Rockstar hasn’t commented on this video, and no outlet has independently verified it. Even reports on the file size don’t agree. What we can say is that it lines up with the leaker’s earlier clips, and parts of it clearly look unfinished.

So treat everything in it as “maybe, in some form, someday,” not as a preview of the final game.

GTA 6 release date, price and preload: the confirmed facts

Leaks are noise. Here’s what Rockstar and Take-Two have actually confirmed:

Release dateNovember 19, 2026
PlatformsPS5 and Xbox Series X|S (no PC version announced)
PreloadStarts November 12
Standard Edition$79.99
Ultimate Edition$99.99, with extra vehicles, weapons, outfits and some edition-only in-game shops
Pre-order bonusVintage Vice City Pack
Physical copiesA download code in the box, not a disc

Yes, $80 is a lot, and the Ultimate Edition locking some in-game shops behind $100 has fans annoyed. You can upgrade to Ultimate after launch, so there’s no rush to decide today.

What else we know about Leonida

If you want new GTA 6 details without spoilers, skip the leak and read Game Informer’s cover story (issue 382, out September 29). It runs 14 pages with 12 new screenshots and interviews with Rockstar’s lead designers. Coverage of the feature highlights:

  • Real Florida weather: hurricanes and tropical storms, not just postcard sunshine.
  • A bigger map: Leonida is reportedly about twice the size of GTA 5’s map.
  • Wildlife everywhere: reportedly more than 170 animal species.

Should you watch the leak?

Personally? I’m not. We’re six weeks out. The footage is unfinished, it spoils the story, and every view rewards someone who cashed out a crypto coin on fans. Rockstar has been doing this for a long time; the version on November 19 will look better than any leaked dev build.

As someone who’s spent way too many hours in GTA RP, I want to go in fresh. If you feel the same, mute “GTA 6 leak” on your socials for a few weeks. Trust me.

FAQ

Is the Cyberleek GTA 6 leak real?

Nobody has verified it. Rockstar hasn’t commented on the October 8 video, and parts of it appear to come from an in-development build, so treat anything in it as unconfirmed.

Does GTA 6 have a first-person mode?

Not a full one at launch, according to Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson. You’ll still be able to use a first-person view when aiming some weapons. The leaked footage shows first-person sections, but it’s from a development build.

When does GTA 6 come out?

November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Preloading is set to start November 12. No PC version has been announced.

How much does GTA 6 cost?

$79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition (US prices). Boxed copies come with a download code instead of a disc.
