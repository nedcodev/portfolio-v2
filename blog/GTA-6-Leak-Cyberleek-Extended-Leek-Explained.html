GTA 6 Leaks Again: Cyberleek Drops 25 Minutes of Gameplay

By Ned · October 9, 2026 · 2 min read

Six weeks before launch, GTA 6 just leaked. Again. And this time it’s 25 minutes long.

What dropped

On October 8, the leaker Cyberleek posted a file called “GTA 6: An Extended Leek.” It’s about 25 minutes of footage, the longest drop yet. It reportedly shows Jason and Lucia in gameplay, plus story spoilers and mature content.

The big one: first-person

The clip that has everyone talking shows first-person driving and walking. Problem: Rockstar has already said there’s no full first-person mode at launch. Some clips also show a developer menu (yes, someone spawns a tank), so this looks like an unfinished build. Features in dev builds change all the time.

Is it real?

Nobody knows yet. Rockstar hasn’t said a word, and no outlet has verified it. Treat everything in it as “maybe.”

Who is Cyberleek?

An anonymous leaker who started posting GTA 6 clips in August, calling it a protest against pre-orders and digital-only games. The twist: the leaks were tied to a crypto coin, and around $250,000 was cashed out before it crashed. Take-Two has reportedly gone to court to unmask them.

My take

I’m not watching it. It’s half-finished and full of spoilers, and the real game is like six weeks away. I’d rather go in blind. If you’re the same, mute “GTA 6 leak” for a bit.

FAQ

Is the GTA 6 leak real?

It’s not verified. Rockstar hasn’t commented on the October 8 video, and parts of it look like an unfinished development build.

Who is Cyberleek?

An anonymous leaker who started posting GTA 6 clips in August 2026 and tied them to a crypto token that crashed after about $250,000 was cashed out.

Does the leak confirm a first-person mode?

No. It shows first-person footage from a development build, but Rockstar has said there’s no full first-person mode at launch.
