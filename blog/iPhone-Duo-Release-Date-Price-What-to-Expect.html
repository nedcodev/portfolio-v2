iPhone Duo: Release Date, Price and What to Expect

By Ned · October 9, 2026 · 3 min read

Apple’s first foldable iPhone is one week away. The iPhone Duo opens for pre-order next Friday, and it isn’t cheap.

When you can get it

Apple announced the iPhone Duo at its September 9 event, alongside the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max. Pre-orders open Friday, October 16 (5 a.m. Pacific, according to MacRumors) in more than 70 countries, and it hits stores Friday, October 23.

MacRumors expects initial stock to be limited, so if you want one on day one, be ready when pre-orders open.

How much the iPhone Duo costs

Tom’s Guide calls it the most expensive iPhone Apple sells. For comparison, Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold 8 starts at $1,899.

Open, it’s the thinnest iPhone ever, beating the iPhone Air. It runs on the A20 Pro chip, has two 48MP cameras (main and ultra-wide), two batteries and IP68 water resistance.

What it’s missing

A few things got cut to make it fold. There’s no Face ID (Touch ID lives in the side button instead), no telephoto camera and no Action Button.

So, is there a crease?

Early hands-ons agree it’s far less visible than on other foldables. TechRadar says you can feel it but not see it. The big unknown is time: Engadget points out nobody knows yet how it will look after months of folding. Apple says the screen’s top layer is replaceable, and 9to5Mac reports that swap costs $19 with AppleCare.

My take

I use an iPhone Air, and the Duo opened up is even thinner, so I get the hype. But $2,000 for Apple’s first try at a foldable is a lot.

I’d wait for the real reviews and see how that crease looks after a few months. Gen two is usually the one to get.

FAQ

When can I pre-order the iPhone Duo?

Pre-orders open Friday, October 16, at 5 a.m. Pacific. It goes on sale Friday, October 23.

How much does the iPhone Duo cost?

It starts at $1,999 for 256GB. The 512GB model is $2,199, 1TB is $2,599 and 2TB is $3,199 in the US.

Does the iPhone Duo have Face ID?

No. It uses Touch ID in the side button, which works whether the phone is open or closed.

Is the iPhone Duo crease visible?

Barely, according to early hands-ons. It’s much less noticeable than on other foldables, but how it holds up over time is still unknown.
